# Future WayMakers Marketplace Disclaimer

**Date last modified:** 19 April 2026

## 1. Marketplace Nature of Service
Future WayMakers (ShapaCV) is a pure marketplace and technological intermediary. We provide the infrastructure for "Employers" (Hirers) to discover and contract "Talent" (Independent Contractors). 

## 2. Independent Contractor Status
By using this platform, all users acknowledge and agree that:
- Talent are **Independent Contractors** and not employees of Future WayMakers or the Employer by default of platform use.
- Future WayMakers does not direct, supervise, or control the work performed.
- Talent are responsible for their own tools, equipment, and tax liabilities (SARS).

## 3. Presumption of Employment (SA Labour Law)
This platform is intentionally structured to fall outside the scope of **Amendment 50A** of the Labour Relations Act. Work contracted through this platform is generally short-term, task-based, and decentralized. Any user seeking to establish an employment relationship must do so via a separate, explicit employment contract outside of this marketplace.

## 4. No Agency
No agency, partnership, joint venture, or employment is created as a result of the Terms of Service or your use of the Platform.
